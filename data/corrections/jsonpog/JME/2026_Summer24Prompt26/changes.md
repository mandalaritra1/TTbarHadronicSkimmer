## Changes: 2026-07-15 (\[Summer24Prompt26\] First JEC and JER recommendations for 2026)

Merge Request: [!1](https://gitlab.cern.ch/cms-analysis-corrections/JME/Run3-26Prompt-Summer24-NanoAODv15/-/merge_requests/1)

In this MR, the first JEC and JER recommendations for 2026 are provided. Within the `jet_jerc.json.gz` and `fatJet_jerc.json.gz` files, the tag names are

- JEC: `Summer24Prompt26_V1_MC`, `Summer24Prompt26_V1_DATA`
- JER: `Summer24Prompt26_JRV1_MC`

**Please note:** The era 2026 C is a dedicated low-PU run. Preliminary JEC and JER for this era are also included in the files above.

The `jetid.json.gz` file is copied from 2025 ([Run3-25Prompt-Summer24-NanoAODv15](https://gitlab.cern.ch/cms-analysis-corrections/JME/Run3-25Prompt-Summer24-NanoAODv15)).

The jet veto map is also cloned from 2025, under the tag name `Summer24Prompt26_RunBCD_V1`.

Corresponds to [JECDatabase PR #228](https://github.com/cms-jet/JECDatabase/pull/228) and [JRDatabase PR #31](https://github.com/cms-jet/JRDatabase/pull/31).
